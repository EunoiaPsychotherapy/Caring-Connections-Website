A little about me

About me

I’m an integrative counsellor with a person-centred foundation. The relationship we build together is at the heart of how I work.

I offer a warm, genuine and non-judgemental space where you can talk openly about what you’re experiencing, at a pace that feels right for you. Rather than taking a one-size-fits-all approach, I draw on different therapeutic ideas depending on what you need and what feels most helpful.

My counselling experience includes more than 100 hours of face-to-face client work with Macmillan at the Delamere Centre, supporting people affected by cancer, bereavement, anticipatory grief and life-changing health conditions. I also volunteer within a counselling hub, supporting people to access affordable therapy.

Alongside my counselling work, I have a background in NHS management and experience working within healthcare environments.

A gentle starting point

Understanding difficult experiences

My own life experiences have also shaped the way I work. I understand that grief, trauma, family difficulties and major life changes can affect us in ways that are not always easy to put into words.

This helps me bring empathy, patience and sensitivity to the counselling relationship, while always keeping the focus on your experience rather than my own.

I have a particular interest in grief and bereavement, including the loss of an adult child, adjusting to life-changing diagnoses, body image, and the ways our early relationships can influence how we feel and connect with others later in life.

A particular area of care

Grief, loss & health changes

Some losses are named. Others arrive quietly, through illness, waiting, or a future that no longer looks the way it did. You may be holding practical decisions alongside feelings that are difficult to speak — love, fear, anger, numbness, or a sense that ordinary life has been quietly rearranged.

I have particular experience supporting people affected by cancer, bereavement and life-changing health conditions through my work with Macmillan at the Delamere Centre. This is not about rushing towards acceptance. It is about having somewhere to bring what is true for you, including the parts that feel unfinished or hard to hold alone.

People often come to me when they are living with:

  • Bereavement
  • Anticipatory grief
  • The loss of an adult child
  • Cancer
  • Life-changing diagnoses
  • Long-term health conditions
  • Existential concerns
  • Body image following health changes

Training and practice

Qualifications & experience

My work is grounded in integrative training, regular supervision, and time spent alongside people at some of the most tender points in their lives.

  • Foundation Degree in Counselling (Integrative) Completed June 2026
  • Level 3 Counselling Skills
  • Level 2 Counselling Skills
  • Certificate in Online and Telephone Counselling

I have completed more than 100 hours of face-to-face counselling experience with Macmillan Cancer Support at the Delamere Centre, Walton Hospital, supporting people affected by cancer, bereavement, anticipatory grief, trauma, anxiety, depression and life-limiting illness.
